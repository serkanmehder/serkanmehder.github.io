I am a fourth-year PhD student in Operations Management at the Leeds School of Business, University of Colorado Boulder.

My research focuses on consumer behavior and decision-making on digital platforms. I study how the way platforms present and update information shapes consumer choices. I use field experiments, causal inference, and statistical modeling to examine these questions, working with platform and clickstream data. My current research includes collaborations with Expedia Group’s Vrbo and 1000Farmacie.

Before starting my PhD, I received a B.A. in Economics from Bilkent University in 2023.
